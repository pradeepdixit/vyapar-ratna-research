# NIFTY 200 mapping basis

The My Stock Selection List uses the official NIFTY 200 constituent snapshot and preserves the source industry, symbol, series and ISIN. The primary source is the Nifty Indices/NSE constituent CSV, updated 28 Oct 2026.

Material tags are research labels for matching the supplied Book of Vyapar Ratna rules. They are not exchange classifications and are not investment advice. Explicit product overrides are used where the company name/business is sufficiently specific (for example steel, cement, cable, gas, pharmaceuticals, tyres, jewellery, solar and power). Rows without an explicit product override retain an industry-level broad mapping and are shown as such in the page.

The page separates minute/instant, daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly and yearly rule counts. Active computable rules are recalculated from the selected astronomy date/time; the duration shown is the book timeframe, while an exact end timestamp requires range sampling.

Source: https://www.niftyindices.com/IndexConstituent/ind_nifty200list.csv
